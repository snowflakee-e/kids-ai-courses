# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Parents of kids aged 6 to 17 are the buyers. They land from Instagram or TikTok ads on a phone, often in the evening, with one question: is this worth my child's time and my money, and is it safe? Kids are the learners, not the readers of the sales page.

## Product Purpose
Bloop AI School teaches kids to use AI well: what AI is (data, models, prompts), how it works, how to use it for homework, creativity and hobbies, and how to stay safe and think critically. Success for the parent page is a booked free trial lesson. This page is the school's main page (the site root): the home of the online AI courses.

## Positioning
One habit loop for every age: Choose, Tell, Check, Own. Every lesson leaves proof a parent can see (Made, How, Think), and the parent report shows the four habits as a meter. Kids never chat with AI alone: tool rules are written per age track.

## Operating Context
Age tracks (confirmed 2026-10-08): 6-8 Explorers, 9-11 Inventors, 12-14 Creators, 15-17 Pros. A 6-year-old and a 16-year-old are different audiences: never one text for all. Each parent page must answer that age's main worry first. Curriculum source: `kids-courses/choose-tell-check-own/curriculum.md`. Course list: `catalog.js` (its group is still `14-17`; the page uses 15-17).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Tool rules: 6-8 a grown-up at the keyboard, mostly unplugged or Bloop simulator; 9-11 no personal chatbot accounts, simulator or parent runs the tool; 12-14 parent account at 12, teen accounts only with consent at 13-14. Claude is 18+ and never used by kids.
- Free first lesson. Prices are not decided: any price shown is a labeled placeholder.
- Language: English only.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Bloop AI School. Mascot: Bloop (the main site still calls him Bobik in places; Bloop is the course name).
- Tone: friendly, concrete, no jargon without an explanation, one idea per block.
- Our AI use statement: "We use AI to help draft lesson ideas and pictures. A human teacher writes the final lessons, checks every fact, and decides what kids see. Kids never chat with AI alone."

## Evidence on Hand
Curriculum with real lesson names, activities and parent at-home cards per track. No real testimonials, enrollment numbers, ratings or prices yet: do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Answer the parent's worry for this age before selling anything.
2. Show proof of learning, not promises.
3. Safety rules are a feature, put them up front.
4. The school grows with the child: same habits, deeper each year.

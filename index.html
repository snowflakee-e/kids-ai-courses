    Frequently asked questions

    What age can my child start at?

    From 6. Younger kids get game-based courses with little text; older kids work on projects and coding.

    Is it safe? Will my child talk to AI directly?

    Kids work in a learning sandbox with content filters. The first module of every course covers online safety rules.

    What do we need for the lessons?

    A computer or tablet, an internet connection and a good mood. Everything else is on the platform.

    How will I know how my child is doing?

    Progress, rewards and projects are visible in the personal cabinet. In group and one-on-one formats the teacher also sends a report.

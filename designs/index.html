Picture Book

A risograph-printed children’s book: blue ink, fluorescent pink and yellow, playful lettering and serif reading text.

Picking an age: a height chart. Tap a band (6–8) or one age (7). On a phone it turns into a vertical doorframe ruler.

Maker Kit

An instruction manual for a build kit: graph paper, thick outlines, buttons that press in, a workshop-rules poster.

Picking an age: four chunky track buttons. The sign-up form narrows its course list to the chosen age.

Calm Lab

Quiet and premium, written for parents: sage and silver, layered cards, a floating menu, a bento of promises.

Picking an age: one sentence, “My child is 9 to 11 and loves drawing”, which filters by age and interest.

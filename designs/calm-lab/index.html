    Questions parents ask.

    Which format should we choose?

    Mini groups of 3 to 6 for younger kids, team groups of 8 to 12 from age 11, one-on-one for any age, and family lessons for a parent with a 6–8-year-old. Teens 14–17 learn in an app with a weekly live hour.

    How do you group kids?

    Never more than two years apart, and one year for the youngest. A short chat before the first lesson checks reading, past AI use and interests.

    Won’t AI do their homework?

    We teach AI as a coach, not an author: it asks questions, your child answers, it explains mistakes. Kids learn how to say where AI helped.

    What does my child need?

    A computer or tablet and about 25 to 50 minutes, depending on age. The youngest work mostly off-screen with cards and games.

    Try one lesson, free.

    Sit with your child for one lesson and see what they make. A teacher replies within a day with times.

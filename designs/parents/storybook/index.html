Questions parents ask first.

Is it safe for a 6 year old?

A grown-up is at the keyboard. Most lessons are unplugged or run inside Bloop's own simulator.

Does my child sit at a screen all hour?

No. Lessons use cards, games and talking. The screen is a tool, not the lesson.

What does my child bring home?

Something they made, like sorted cards or a stamped story page, plus one sentence in their own words.

What if my child is 9?

Pick You're the Boss of AI, made for ages 9 to 11, with experiments instead of stories.

How we use AIWe use AI to help draft lesson ideas and pictures. A human teacher writes the final lessons, checks every fact, and decides what kids see. Kids never chat with AI alone.

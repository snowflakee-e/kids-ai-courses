      What parents ask first.

      Is my child safe with AI?

      Kids do not chat with AI alone. At 6 to 8 a grown-up sits at the keyboard. At 9 to 11 kids use a built-in simulator with prepared answers, or a parent runs the tool.

      Will this make my child lazy or help them cheat?

      The opposite is the plan. Kids learn to ask for a hint instead of the answer, to check every claim and to say when AI helped.

      My child is 9. Which course?

      You're the Boss of AI, for ages 9 to 11. The free lesson shows whether the level feels right before you pay.

      Does my child need an account on an AI tool?

      Not under 12. At 12 a parent's account is used with the parent in the room. At 13 and 14 a teen account is used only with your consent.

      What do I get after a class?

      A short report with something your child made, how they got there, what they think, and one thing to try at home.

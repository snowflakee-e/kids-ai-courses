What parents of teens ask first.

Straight answers, taken from the lessons themselves.

Will my teen just make AI do the homework?

They practice the opposite. In lesson five they set up a study buddy that quizzes and gives hints, then try to trick it into giving answers and fix the setup.

How will I know they learned anything?

Every lesson leaves proof: something made, a log of how they got there, or a reflection. You see all three by demo day.

What happens to their data?

In lesson eight your teen reads a real tool's privacy settings with you, redacts a sample text and turns off training on data together with you.

Will AI make their thinking lazy?

They verify claims in two sources, compare tools and write rules for when not to use AI at all.

Assembly instructions for every lesson

Five steps, the same at 6 and at 16. The parts change with age; the order doesn’t.

  1. 1

    Spot the puzzle

    A riddle from their world: why does the Minecraft zombie walk to you, not into the wall?

  2. 2

    Predict

    Before anything runs, your child bets on what the AI will do.

  3. 3

    Test it

    Train, prompt or sort. One short task, 1 to 5 minutes.

  4. 4

    Debug

    The result surprises them. With Bloop they find the reason: missing data, a vague prompt, a made-up fact.

  5. 5

    Ship it

    A picture, a bot, a game level. Signed with what they did and what the AI did.

Then the lesson ends. Bloop recharges until tomorrow.

Four tracks, one spiral

The same big ideas come back every year, a level deeper: at 6 Bloop learns to tell toys apart, at 15 your teen builds a study bot.

Workshop rules

Printed for kids, written for parents.

  • Bloop says it’s a program. At the start of every chat, and again if a kid forgets.
  • No names, schools or photos go in. Bloop hides them and explains why.
  • Under 13, Bloop’s sandbox only. No public chatbots. From 13, real tools with your consent.
  • Parents set the daily limit. When it’s reached, the next lesson waits until tomorrow.
  • Stars for discoveries. Not for minutes on screen, not for just logging in.
  • Nothing burns. No daily streaks, no loot boxes, no notifications to kids.
